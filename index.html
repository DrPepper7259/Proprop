How it projects: recent per-minute production (60% last 10 games, 40% season) times projected minutes, adjusted for the opponent’s defense against that stat and the expected game pace, with a normal curve built from recent game-to-game swings. Early in the season those adjustments are shrunk toward zero because samples are small. Injuries still need to be entered by hand in injuries.json. The score is a ranking aid, not a guarantee, and it has not been backtested.
